## Rules for this log

- Entries are append-only.
- A correction uses a new Decision ID and explicitly supersedes the earlier entry.
- A global decision is a working constraint, not automatically a frozen standard or effective company policy.
- Domain implementation details remain in the relevant domain records.
- A Candidate model may be used for design and decision preparation but does not create named appointments, technical permissions or operational authority.
