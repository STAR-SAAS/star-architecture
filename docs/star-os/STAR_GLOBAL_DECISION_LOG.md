## Rules for this log

- Entries are append-only.
- A correction uses a new Decision ID and explicitly supersedes the earlier entry.
- A confirmed leadership decision is a working constraint, not automatically a frozen standard or effective company policy.
- Domain implementation details remain in the relevant domain records.
- Candidate models may support design and decision preparation but do not create named appointments, technical permissions or operational authority.

## Decision status distinctions

- Named appointments or Authority-holder appointments: **None**.
- Numeric monetary, commercial, risk, delegation-duration and emergency-duration thresholds: **Missing**.
