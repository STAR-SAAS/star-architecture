## Purpose

PDAP is the architecture-first, founder-led and time-boxed program that governs readiness before Shared Foundation engineering and formal SmartQuote or GateHub development may begin.

## Authority

- Global covenant: `GDEC-0012`
- Master program: `PDAP_MASTER_PROGRAM.md`
- Readiness gates: `PDAP_ARCHITECTURE_READINESS_GATES.md`
- Readiness matrix: `PDAP_ARCHITECTURE_READINESS_MATRIX.md`
- Runtime memory: `runtime/CONTEXT_PACKAGE.md`

## Boundaries

PDAP does not itself approve a technology stack, product architecture, Product Commitment, engineering start or Delivery start. Professional product and technical work remains in the applicable professional scope.
