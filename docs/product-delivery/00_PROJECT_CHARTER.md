## Why this work exists

STAR needs a durable, easy-to-understand way to turn customer and user needs into valuable, reliable product outcomes while coordinating product, project, architecture, engineering, testing, AI, operations and governance teams.

## Current objective

Build a lightweight, evidence-based Product Delivery foundation that:

- keeps decisions and knowledge traceable;
- makes each role's next action clear;
- supports human and AI collaboration;
- reuses mature industry methods instead of recreating them;
- remains understandable to new team members;
- connects work to customer or user value and operational feedback.

## Current scope

- Product Delivery working method and governance
- Decision and knowledge persistence in Markdown
- Research Canon and source verification
- Stakeholder and role coverage
- Mission, work, artifact and lifecycle concepts
- Team-readable documentation design
- GitHub repository strategy for versioned product and engineering knowledge

## Out of scope for this phase

- Replacing GitHub, issue trackers, CRM, monitoring, ERP or other mature tools
- Final implementation architecture for SmartQuote or GateHub
- A complete enterprise ontology or knowledge graph
- A final STAR product name or commercial positioning
- Treating research hypotheses as frozen standards

## Non-negotiable constraints

1. **Understandability:** useful content must be easy to read and apply.
2. **Customer and user value:** internal activity must trace to a value receiver.
3. **Cross-functional coverage:** design must include all affected teams and external participants.
4. **Evidence:** mature standards and primary sources take priority over personal preference.
5. **Traceability:** confirmed decisions must be recorded in Markdown.
6. **Progressive disclosure:** people see the detail required for their work, not the entire theory.
7. **Validation before freeze:** no important model is frozen merely because it sounds convincing.

## Success criteria

- A new team member can find the relevant guidance without reading the full research history.
- A product manager, project manager, architect, developer, QA engineer, AI team member and operations engineer can identify their next action from a Mission view.
- Customer and end-user impact is visible in planning and acceptance.
- Every material decision has an ID, rationale, status and impact.
- New proposals are checked against prior decisions before acceptance.
- GitHub state is stated accurately; no file is described as synchronized unless it has actually been pushed.
