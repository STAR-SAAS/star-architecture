# STAR Stakeholder Map

**Version:** v0.1.0  
**Status:** Coverage baseline; individual Mission analysis still required

A role list is not enough. Each Mission must identify direct users, value receivers, workers, owners, approvers, maintainers, integrators and affected external parties.

## 1. Value receivers and external users

- Prospects and buyers
- Contracting customers
- Customer administrators
- End users
- Merchants and merchant operators
- Internal customers using shared platforms or services
- Accessibility and assistive-technology users

## 2. External ecosystem

- Channel and referral partners
- Implementation partners and system integrators
- Technology partners and ISVs
- Banks, PSPs, acquirers, issuers and card schemes where relevant
- Vendors, suppliers and cloud/service providers
- Regulators, auditors and certification bodies
- Investors, shareholders and board representatives
- Open-source users, contributors and maintainers where applicable

## 3. Leadership and business

- Board, CEO, COO, CTO/CIO, CPO and CFO
- Business development and partnerships
- Sales and presales
- Marketing and growth
- Account management
- Customer success and merchant success
- Commercial and pricing teams

## 4. Product and delivery

- Product manager / product owner
- Business analyst
- UX researcher, service designer and UI/UX designer
- Project manager, program manager and PMO
- Delivery manager / scrum master where used
- Solution architect, software architect and enterprise architect
- Technical writer and enablement/training owner

## 5. Engineering and data

- Frontend, backend and mobile engineers
- Tech leads and engineering managers
- QA, test automation, performance and security testers
- AI/ML engineers, data scientists and evaluation specialists
- Data engineers, analytics engineers and DBAs
- Platform engineers and developer experience teams
- DevOps, SRE and release engineers
- Security engineers and application security

## 6. Operations and support

- Customer support and technical support
- Implementation and onboarding teams
- NOC/SOC and incident response
- Merchant/payment/risk/fraud operations
- Service owners and on-call responders
- Training and knowledge enablement

## 7. Governance and corporate support

- Legal and privacy
- Compliance and regulatory affairs
- Risk management
- Internal audit
- Finance, tax and treasury
- Procurement and vendor management
- HR, recruitment and people operations
- Business continuity and disaster recovery

## 8. AI and system actors

AI actors may include product analysis, architecture, coding, testing, review, documentation, support and operations agents. System actors may include:

- GitHub and source control
- Issue/project tracking
- CI/CD and artifact registries
- Cloud and runtime platforms
- Monitoring, logging and incident tools
- CRM, ERP, finance and support systems
- Email, chat, calendar and document platforms
- Identity, access and secrets systems
- Customer and partner APIs

## Mandatory Mission stakeholder questions

1. Who receives the value?
2. Who directly uses the outcome?
3. Who performs the work, including AI or systems?
4. Who owns the long-term product/capability/service?
5. Who approves high-risk changes?
6. Who operates and supports the result?
7. Who is indirectly affected?
8. Which external organizations or regulators apply?
9. Which systems are authoritative for the information involved?
10. Which stakeholder has been overlooked because they do not attend the delivery meetings?
