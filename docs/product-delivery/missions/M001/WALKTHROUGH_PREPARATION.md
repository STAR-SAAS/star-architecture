## Walkthrough agenda

1. **Why and value** — validate value receiver, current problem, why now and measurable result.
2. **First-stage boundary** — confirm every in-scope and out-of-scope item is understood.
3. **Acceptance scenario** — walk through Merchant → Opportunity → Quote → Quote Version → Pricing → Approval → Quote Issuance → API Query.
4. **Responsibilities** — each participant states their responsibility, approval boundary and next action.
5. **Dependencies and source inventory** — identify current systems, authoritative records, owners and freshness.
6. **Risk and governance** — review commercial pricing, cost-data access, approval permissions, audit, security, compliance and financial impact.
7. **Evidence plan** — confirm what evidence is required at M2, M3 and M4 and who owns it.
8. **Commitment gate** — record whether the Mission is Ready or Not Ready to become Committed and route to SmartQuote Delivery.

## Questions every participant must answer

- What valuable outcome is this Mission trying to achieve?

- What is your responsibility and authority boundary?
- What is your next action?
- What dependency or decision can block you?
- What evidence proves your part is complete?
- Which source is authoritative for your information?
- What risk would require leadership escalation?

## M1 pass criteria

M1 passes only when:

- Robin and Jason confirm the Mission brief accurately represents the leadership decisions;
- all required participant roles have named representatives;
- one business acceptance representative is named;
- every participant can explain the outcome, boundary, responsibility, next action and evidence without relying on the full research history;
- conflicting, duplicate or stale sources are identified and classified;
- material dependencies, risks, approvals and missing evidence are recorded;
- no participant treats historical discussion, example files or unsupported completion claims as authoritative scope;
- a clear recommendation is recorded on whether the Mission may become Committed;
- leadership retains approval over commitment and routing to SmartQuote Delivery.
