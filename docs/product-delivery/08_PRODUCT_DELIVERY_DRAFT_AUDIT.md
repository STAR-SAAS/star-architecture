# Audit of Early Product Delivery Drafts

**Version:** v0.1.0  
**Audit date:** 2026-07-12  
**Status:** Working audit

The early documents were originally labelled v1.0. They are reclassified here as **draft v0.1 concepts** because they were produced before the current research, stakeholder and readability disciplines were established.

## 1. STAR Product Delivery Standard

**Current status:** Needs restructuring.

### Retain

- common delivery principles;
- clear ownership;
- Definition of Ready / Definition of Done concepts;
- traceability and governance intent;
- reusable, configurable and API-oriented engineering guidance where relevant.

### Revise

- separate enduring product/capability structure from temporary delivery structure;
- avoid mandatory Scrum vocabulary as the universal model;
- include customer/end-user value and operations feedback;
- make role and stakeholder impact explicit;
- reduce generic statements that cannot be tested.

## 2. STAR Product Delivery Playbook

**Current status:** Useful skeleton; major simplification required.

### Retain

- discovery, planning, design, development, testing, UAT, release and operation concerns;
- decision gates;
- role accountability;
- release, rollback and operational readiness.

### Revise

- do not require every change to follow the same heavyweight sequence;
- use risk/complexity to select the minimum process;
- add customer outcome and post-release learning;
- define what information each role needs rather than exposing one long playbook;
- include AI authority and validation boundaries.

## 3. STAR Product Artifact Standard

**Current status:** Directionally useful; subordinate to work and decisions.

### Retain

- IDs, owners, status, version and revision history;
- links among requirements, decisions, API/data, tests and releases;
- archived/superseded states;
- repository organization and change traceability.

### Revise

- an artifact is a representation, not the core truth by itself;
- do not generate documents merely to satisfy a catalogue;
- use minimum required artifacts by risk and stakeholder need;
- provide concise role-facing views and machine-readable source formats where useful.

## Audit conclusion

Do not publish the three early drafts as frozen v1.0 standards. Preserve their useful concepts, then rebuild a smaller Product Delivery baseline after Mission and minimal object/lifecycle questions are validated on SmartQuote and GateHub.
