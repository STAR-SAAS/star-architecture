# STAR Superseded and Rejected Ideas

**Version:** v0.1.0  
**Status:** Active traceability record

This file prevents circular discussion. "Superseded" means a later view is currently preferred; it does not erase history.

| ID | Earlier idea | Status | Why it changed | Current replacement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SUP-001 | Document = knowledge | Rejected | Documents are containers; decisions, rules and models may exist in multiple representations. | Manage authoritative meaning, status and traceability; use documents as views. |
| SUP-002 | GitHub is the universal single source of truth for the enterprise | Rejected | Tasks, monitoring, CRM, finance and communication have different authoritative systems. | Define one authoritative source per information class; GitHub leads for versioned product/engineering knowledge. |
| SUP-003 | Product → Mission → Epic → Feature → Story as one universal hierarchy | Superseded | Mixes enduring product structure with temporary delivery structure and assumes Scrum terminology. | Validate a smaller value/Mission/work model with separate product capability views. |
| SUP-004 | All important knowledge should be exposed directly to every team member | Rejected | Creates cognitive overload and slow onboarding. | Progressive disclosure and audience-specific views. |
| SUP-005 | Architecture first, then adapt users and teams to it | Rejected | Produces elegant internal models that may not support actual work or customer value. | Start with problem, value receiver, stakeholders and work; architecture supports delivery. |
| SUP-006 | Research report is the primary team deliverable | Rejected | Research is valuable to maintainers but usually too broad for execution teams. | Translate research into one-page summaries, handbooks, specifications and role-relevant context. |
| SUP-007 | STAR should invent replacements for mature methods | Rejected | Increases learning and maintenance cost without proven benefit. | Adopt, adapt and integrate mature practices; innovate only for a verified gap. |
| SUP-008 | STAR = Knowledge OS | Superseded hypothesis | Too narrow; Product Delivery, operations, governance and human/AI work extend beyond knowledge management. | Final STAR definition remains open. |
| SUP-009 | STAR = Meta Architecture | Superseded hypothesis | Describes integration structure but not practical execution or customer outcomes. | Final STAR definition remains open. |
| SUP-010 | STAR = Context Engineering System | Superseded hypothesis | Context is essential for AI but is only one capability. | Context remains a capability/asset candidate. |
| SUP-011 | STAR = Consistency Engine | Superseded hypothesis | Consistency is largely an outcome of ownership, mechanisms, automation and feedback. | Define executable mechanisms and measure outcomes. |
| SUP-012 | STAR = Coordination System | Superseded hypothesis | Coordination is necessary but does not fully express value creation and continuous learning. | Final STAR definition remains open. |
| SUP-013 | Role alone should determine the entire workspace | Superseded hypothesis | One person can perform several kinds of work; work context also matters. | Test a combined role + work + authority view. |
| SUP-014 | AI is merely a generic assistant | Superseded direction | AI teams and agents may perform specialized work, but require governance and human accountability. | Model AI participation explicitly; exact role remains open. |
