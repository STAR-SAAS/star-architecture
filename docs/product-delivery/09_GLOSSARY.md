# STAR Working Glossary

**Version:** v0.1.0  
**Status:** Working definitions; terms marked "Open" are not frozen

| Term | Working definition | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | Person or organization that buys, contracts for or receives a STAR product/service. | Working |
| End User | Person who directly uses the product or service; may differ from the buyer/customer. | Working |
| Value Receiver | Broader candidate term for an external or internal party that receives a meaningful outcome. | Open |
| Stakeholder | Person, team, organization, AI agent or system that affects, performs, governs, consumes or is affected by the work. | Working |
| Product | Enduring offering managed to create outcomes for customers/users over time. | Working |
| Capability | Enduring ability to achieve an outcome, independent of a specific tool or org chart. | Open but high-confidence |
| Mission | Candidate cross-functional container for a bounded valuable outcome. Exact size and hierarchy remain open. | Open |
| Work | Activity required to achieve an outcome; may be performed by humans, AI or systems. | Open |
| Decision | Explicit choice with context, alternatives, rationale, owner, consequences and lifecycle. | Working |
| ADR | Decision Record for an architecturally significant choice. | Working |
| Artifact | A representation or deliverable produced or maintained during work, such as a specification, diagram, test or release note. | Working |
| Knowledge | Validated, reusable and traceable understanding; not synonymous with a file. | Open |
| Context | Current, relevant and authorized information needed by a person or AI to perform a specific task. | Open |
| Owner | Accountable party for an enduring product, capability, service, decision or knowledge item. | Working |
| Worker | Candidate generic term for a human, AI or system performing work. | Open |
| Authority | Permission to decide, approve, change or act within a defined boundary. | Open |
| Research Canon | Reviewed catalogue of external methods/standards and STAR's minimum adoption posture. | Working |
| Frozen | Formally approved stable baseline; changes require an explicit superseding decision. | Working |

## Naming rule

Do not create a new STAR term when an established term is adequate. A new term requires a clear gap, definition, examples, non-examples and an owner.
